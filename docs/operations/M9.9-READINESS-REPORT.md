## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone M9.9 certifies the operational readiness of Kelvin across its deployed production environment. Through independent deployment auditing, existing custom URL verification, worker daemon hardening, and controlled lab target qualifications, Kelvin is verified to satisfy all security and architectural requirements for real scanning execution.

The platform architecture strictly separates control plane operations from execution sandboxes. In accordance with safety policies, the worker daemon remains in an inactive standby state, and `KELVIN_CANARY_ONLY=true` is maintained until explicit human activation authorization is granted.
